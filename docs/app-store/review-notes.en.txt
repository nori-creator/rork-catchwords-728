CatchWords helps people learn Taiwanese Mandarin (Traditional Chinese) words from photos of everyday things. Free, no in-app purchases, no ads.

SIGN IN
Use the demo account in "Sign-in information" above: Sign in > "Continue with email". It has finished the first-run setup and the AI consent and already has words. Sign in with Apple and Google are also offered.

MAIN FLOW
1. Camera tab: photograph any object (cup, bottle, chair), or pick a photo with the Photos button. Word tags appear on the objects after a few seconds.
2. Tap a word: its sticker (cut out of the photo), the word and its pronunciation appear, and the word is added to the collection. A word that is not shown can be looked up with "Type a different word".
3. Collection tab: tap a word for zhuyin, pinyin, meaning, pronunciation (speaker button) and example sentences.
4. Review tab: 4-choice questions with the user's own photos.
5. Settings: languages, level, notifications, Legal (Terms, Privacy Policy, consent to send data to AI), Sign out, and Delete account at the bottom (deletes the account and its data on our server).

DATA AND AI
Photos and typed words go only to our server (catchwords.lovable.app). Our server sends them to a third-party AI service (Google Gemini, with Lovable AI Gateway relaying to Gemini as a backup; the operator may switch a feature to another provider named in the privacy policy, e.g. Anthropic Claude) only after the user agrees on the in-app screen "Sending data to AI", shown before the first AI feature. Without consent nothing is sent and the camera stays off; collected words and review still work. Consent can be withdrawn in Settings > Legal. Pronunciation of Taiwanese Mandarin: Microsoft Azure AI Speech (text only). Optional replacement photos for a word: Unsplash / Wikimedia Commons, with credit.

WHY SIGN-IN IS REQUIRED
Photos, collected words and review history are saved per account on our server, and the AI features have a per-account daily limit to prevent abuse, so almost nothing works without an account. The same account works on the CatchWords website.

OTHER
- Camera, photo library, location and notification permissions are optional and asked only when the feature is used.
- Some diagnostic screens in Settings are shown only to the developer's own account; they do not appear for other users or the demo account.
- The app works the same in every territory where it is offered.
